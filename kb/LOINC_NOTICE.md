# LOINC notice

`loinc_subset.csv` holds a small hand-picked subset of LOINC codes (about 45 common lab tests, with similar-looking codes next to each other on purpose: serum vs blood vs urine, troponin I vs T, standard vs high-sensitivity CRP) so the mapping agent has to choose, not just match a name.

This material contains content from LOINC (https://loinc.org). LOINC is copyright © Regenstrief Institute, Inc. and the Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes (LOINC) Committee and is available at no cost under the license at https://loinc.org/license.

The subset is for demonstration only. Check codes against the current LOINC release before using them in production.
